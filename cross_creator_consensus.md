## ⚔️ 2. Divergent Frameworks & Contradictions

### ⚖️ Productivity: Out-Working vs. Automating & Eliminating
- **Alex Hormozi (Out-Work the Competition):** Execute 100 primary daily actions without seeking shortcuts. Volume negates bad luck.
- **Tim Ferriss (Minimum Effective Dose & 80/20 Elimination):** Identify the 20% of tasks producing 80% of results. Eliminate, automate, or delegate the remaining 80%.
🎯 **Synthesis Resolution:** Use Tim Ferriss's elimination framework to select the single highest-leverage task, then apply Alex Hormozi's extreme volume to execute that task.

### ⚖️ Mindset: Behavioral Repetition vs. Emotional Processing
- **ThePrimeagen / Technical Mentors (Brute-Force Muscle Memory):** Repetition in terminal/editor and cold exposure to hard problems builds resilience.
- **Dr. K (HealthyGamerGG) / Joe Hudson (Unprocessed Trauma & Samskara Resolution):** Forcing action without processing underlying emotional scars leads to inevitable burnout and executive dysfunction.
🎯 **Synthesis Resolution:** Process underlying emotional resistance (Dr. K) before engaging in intensive repetition routines to avoid friction.
